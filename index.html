Aadya Kanchan - Maker Portfolio

Hi! I’m glad you could stop by. I like working on water and health problems by starting with the thing that is not working, building a rough version, and then changing it after tests in the real world. These are a few projects that taught me to pay attention to small details: a gear that might buckle, a drain shape that changes a vortex, or a light signal that separates one sample from another.

Projects

A NIRI device showing the gear mechanism that turns the valve.

NIRI

An IoT-enabled water conservation system that I kept rebuilding: from relays and a potentiometer to a servo-driven gear system with a ball valve. I tested the device at a school canteen, homes, and hospitals.

A 3D-printed AquaFlux vortex device with an adapter for a parallel grate inlet.

AquaFlux

A passive drainage insert that uses vortex geometry rather than power. I studied waterlogging sites, compared geometries in ANSYS, printed versions, and tested them at different inflow and turbidity conditions.

Assembling the light-isolated testing setup for Arogya Dx.

Arogya Dx

A point-of-care lipid-screening prototype that reads plasma spectral signatures. I worked through microfluidic samples, a light-isolated chamber, optical sensors, and comparisons between concentration curves.

Groundwater mapping in Bangarpet

A GIS-based study of seasonal groundwater changes and recharge potential in a drought-prone town near Bengaluru.
